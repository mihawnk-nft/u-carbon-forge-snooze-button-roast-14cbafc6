Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6) competition **Snooze Button Roast** (Growth, $2 prize pool).

## What it does

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no network, no input) writes one
line — 111 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`: a roast of an alarm clock
that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning.

> At 6 AM on Saturday my alarm clock shrieks like a smoke detector that smelled happiness and came to put it out.

Why this line: a fresh angle (the alarm as a smoke detector, not the tired
"hate mornings" complaint), a concrete image, and the punchline pays off in
the last few words — sayable out loud in one breath.

## How the line was picked

Three candidate angles were weighed against the judge's brief (roast the
6 AM Saturday alarm, fresh angle, punchline at the end): the alarm as a
smoke detector won over the alarm as a drill sergeant and the alarm as a
door-to-door salesman, because the detector gives the sharpest concrete
image and the funniest last words ("came to put it out").

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
